Welcome to GTA VI.

A quick introduction to the game. No story spoilers.

What is GTA VI?

Grand Theft Auto VI is the next entry in the Rockstar Games series. Jason and Lucia travel through Leonida, including Vice City. After a job goes wrong, they become entangled in a criminal conspiracy.

Are Vice City and Leonida the same place?

Vice City is a city. Leonida is the state where the story takes place. Our map covers more than just the city.

Which platforms?

The official announcement lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Check release times for your region separately. We do not assume a PC release date.

How do I use this guide?

Use the knowledge base for general questions, Missions for individual tasks and the Map for locations. Every section has its own page that you can bookmark or share.

Controls and first missions

Verified instructions for controls, saving and early missions are not available yet. We will add them after review, without presenting advice from previous games as GTA VI rules.
